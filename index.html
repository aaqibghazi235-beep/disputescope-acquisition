What the project does today

A focused analytics foundation for reviewing payment transaction and dispute datasets.

01

Import and inspect

Upload transaction CSVs and review parsed transaction records, data quality, and duplicate identifiers.

02

Analyze dispute patterns

Review disputed transactions, dispute reasons, rates, and operational summaries.

03

Reconcile records

Compare transaction datasets, match records, and surface amount differences for review.

04

Explore analytics

Use dashboard views, search, filters, and historical summaries where supported by imported data.

05

Export reports

Export transaction data, findings, and analysis outputs as CSV files.

06

Demonstrate safely

Use synthetic sample records to evaluate the product without exposing real cardholder data.

Important status boundaries

The project is early-stage and pre-revenue. Do not assume the following are live or production-ready without independent verification:

  • Payment gateway integrations and webhooks.
  • Production authentication and tenant isolation.
  • Subscription billing and entitlement enforcement.
  • Automated dispute submission or representment.
  • Validated machine-learning predictions or compliance certifications.
Demonstration safety: Use synthetic data only. Do not upload real cardholder or customer financial data to the public demo.
